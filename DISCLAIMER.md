# Disclaimer

**This software is for education and research. It is not financial advice and
comes with no warranty of profitability.**

- Trading prediction markets and crypto derivatives carries a substantial risk
  of losing **all** capital deployed. Most automated retail strategies lose
  money after fees, slippage, and adverse selection.
- The article that inspired this project describes an idealized, marketed
  result. On-chain "PnL" leaderboards do not show losing wallets, failed bots,
  or the survivorship bias behind any single highlighted account. Treat
  seven-figure claims with skepticism.
- The **demo and backtest use synthetic data**. Their returns are an
  illustration of the mechanics, **not** a prediction of live performance. A
  strategy that prints money on simulated, well-behaved data routinely fails
  live, where the latency gap is small (seconds), competitive, and full of
  adverse selection.
- Latency arbitrage is a genuine, competitive HFT-style game. Without
  co-located infrastructure and sub-50ms execution you are usually the slow
  participant being arbitraged.

  your jurisdiction, and with Polymarket's terms of service, including any

**Default behavior is paper trading.** Live trading requires deliberately
setting three separate environment gates. Do not enable them unless you fully
understand and accept that you may lose everything you risk.
